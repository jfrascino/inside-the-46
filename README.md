# Inside the 46

An interactive, visual atlas of 27 chromosome conditions for middle-school science, built as a 5-step mission: fly from a cell down to its DNA, watch how an extra chromosome can happen, explore 27 conditions animated with real 3D chromosomes, build your own chromosome change in the Lab, and play karyotype games. Includes a teacher guide.

**Open it:** https://jfrascino.github.io/inside-the-46/

The whole app is one self-contained file, so it also works offline: download [`inside-the-46.html`](https://jfrascino.github.io/inside-the-46/inside-the-46.html) and open it in any browser. Computers that can't show 3D automatically get a flat version.

Educational resource only, not medical advice. Every factual claim (more than 1,200) was checked against MedlinePlus Genetics, GeneReviews, the NIH, the CDC, NORD, ClinGen, Ensembl and peer-reviewed papers in September 2026, with an independent second check. The [evidence trail](https://jfrascino.github.io/inside-the-46/evidence.html) lists every claim, the result and its source. Chromosome bands come from the GRCh38 reference genome. 3D rendering uses three.js (MIT license).
