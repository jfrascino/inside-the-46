Inside the 46 · Evidence trail

How every fact was checked

Every sentence in the app that states a fact is listed below, with what happened when it was checked and the source it was checked against. Teachers can use this page to trace any number, date or definition back to where it came from.

The method

  1. Every factual claim in the app was listed: numbers, dates, names, definitions, captions, quiz answers and the notes in the Lab.
  2. Each claim was checked against a named source: MedlinePlus Genetics, GeneReviews, the NIH, the CDC, NORD, ClinGen, Ensembl or a peer-reviewed paper. Where good sources disagree, the app gives a range or follows MedlinePlus Genetics.
  3. Claims the sources did not support were corrected or removed. Corrected claims show the earlier wording underneath.
  4. A second, independent check re-opened the sources for every correction and for a random sample of the confirmed claims.
  5. An independent audit of the live site found further problems, and those were fixed too.

The checks were done with AI assistance in September 2026. They have not been reviewed by a medical geneticist or a genetic counselor, so please report anything that looks wrong. Quotations from the sources are kept off this page for copyright reasons; every link goes to the source that was checked. This page shows each claim as it was checked, and a few sentences were polished afterwards.
