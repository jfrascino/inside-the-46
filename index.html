Inside the 46 is an educational resource for middle-school science. It is not medical advice. Chromosome bands are drawn from the human reference genome (GRCh38, UCSC cytoBand). Facts are drawn from MedlinePlus Genetics (U.S. National Library of Medicine), the CDC, NIH and condition foundations. Sources are listed on each page.

People are more than their chromosomes. Every condition here describes a difference in DNA, never the worth of a person.
